WHY KOREA, WHY NOW

The society that faces the most death,
yet speaks of it least.

Korea is ageing faster than any country on record. Deaths are climbing sharply — and almost no one is prepared to talk about them.
20%
Super-aged society
In 2025 people aged 65+ passed 20% of the population. Annual deaths are rising steeply.
18.7%
Preparation left behind
Even among those aged 65+, fewer than 1 in 5 have completed an advance directive.
54.3%
And yet — silence
More than half of Korean adults have never once talked about death with their family.
OUR APPROACH

Death literacy is a measurable capacity,
not a personality trait.

The Death Literacy Index breaks the ability to face death into four dimensions. We are adapting it for the Korean context as the K-DLI — the country's first.
1
Practical knowledge
Can you hands-on handle caregiving, end-of-life care and funerals?
2
Factual knowledge
Can you navigate the systems — life-sustaining treatment decisions, funerals, inheritance?
3
Experiential knowledge
Have you learned from being close to death and grief, and can you use that learning?
4
Community support
Do you know where to turn, and can your community carry the load with you?
Framework: Noonan et al., Death Literacy Index (2024) · Korean adaptation in progress with La Trobe University and Hallym University Institute of Life and Death Studies.
